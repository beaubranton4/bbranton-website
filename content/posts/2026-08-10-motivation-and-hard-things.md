# August 10, 2026

This morning I woke up and kind of reflected on this year. So much time has gone past — almost one year — and it felt like I really haven't done anything. When I say I haven't done anything, I really think about where I want to be in terms of my goals with solopreneurship, entrepreneurship, and Dugout Edge. Well, I've made great strides and probably have accomplished more than I thought that I could. I've actually surpassed a lot of my goals with Dugout Edge. But I feel like that motivation and that drive has really kind of dissipated in the last month or two. I think it's because life is just so busy. All these trips, all of these fun plans going on. Trying to watch good anime, read good books like the Stormlight Archive. Trying to eat healthy, trying to get in shape, trying to lose body fat. It's so hard to balance everything, and I think I'm doing a good job — but I want to be doing a great job.

I think it all comes down to motivation. That motivation, drive, willingness to be uncomfortable and do the hard things — that comes from doing the hard things. Making the right decisions day in and day out. Not eating the junk food, so that I feel energized throughout the day. Getting that workout in, so that I stay in shape and I look good and I don't want to eat that junk food. Not having those couple of beers at night, so that I get a good night's sleep and I'm well rested for the next day, ready to crush.

In any case, I really need to grind the next couple of weeks. Push the gameday tool, push the practice day tool, so that everything's ready to go by football.

I want to preserve all my interactions with Claude Code ever, even in the case that my computer crashes. I would like to be able to use what I've built locally while on my phone — for example logging, tracking my to-dos, building Dugout Edge. I want everything in one place, accessible from all devices, anywhere in the world.

I'm starting to think this whole protein trend is bad for you. I think people want to start eating healthy and training healthy — not for looks and aesthetics, but for feel and for function.
