# August 9, 2026

Had a really fun time hanging out with Jess and Nick. Watched our very first 4D movie, which was Spider-Man — I had seen Into the Spider-Verse, but this was my first Spider-Man movie with Tom Holland. Thought that was a really good movie; might have to get into that series and that whole Marvel Comics world. That would be a fun kind of thing to get into.

I did have a good run — ran five miles at about 8-minute pace and then walked one mile.

Dinner was Ox and Tiger, an omakase-style Japanese and Filipino inspired restaurant run by a couple who cook the food in front of you, tell you a story, talk to you. Food was delicious, experience was amazing. I'm not sure it was worth the amount that we paid, but a really phenomenal experience nonetheless.
