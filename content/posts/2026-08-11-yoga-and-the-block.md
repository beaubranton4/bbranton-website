# August 11, 2026

I was thinking when I was doing yoga this morning that one thing I miss is doing these group classes. I really like doing them. But the funny thing is, I like the part of doing them with people, but I don't really like interacting or communicating with the people — which is really interesting to me.

Trying to plan a trip for our family in November. Might do Vietnam, which could be kind of fun. Went there as a kid but barely remember it. Would be super cool to go back to my roots, see my people.

Time is really starting to just pass by. It's almost been a year since I launched Dugout Edge. $15,000 in the first year in revenue, call it $12,000 in profit — probably more than that, $13,000 in profit. Pretty darn good for one year. Might be a bit more, maybe $15K in profit. Still not good enough to replace the full-time job though.

What I want to talk about in today's journal entry is just the block with Dugout Edge. Something about it — I've just been losing momentum, losing steam. I don't have the excitement to keep pursuing, keep pushing like I did before. But you know, maybe that's okay. I feel like I'm like some of these other indie hackers where I'm losing steam, losing momentum, and that's totally fine — because the whole point of this is to have fun and enjoy what you're doing at the same time. I think I still am having fun, but it's hard. I think I know what I don't like: I don't like testing the product, trying to look for bugs, making design considerations, and then just telling it what to do. I just want it to work. I have the vision and I want it to work. That's why I love this process of vibe coding, but it's still, at this day in 2026, not good enough where I'm just happy with the results. I really have to babysit it, which kind of takes the fun out of it a little bit.

There are also things that I really know I need to be doing to make the business successful, and that's like creating another automated marketing funnel. Right now SEO is cool and great, but it's getting competitive. I'm getting lazy, I get bored, I don't have the time. I know I need to be making YouTube videos, Instagram videos. I think I'm just waiting for the tool to be built up, but I don't know — the tool is pretty darn built out, pretty complete. Maybe once I build up the features I'll feel the confidence to try everything.

I know that I need to be hiring people. I need to hire someone. I can't be afraid to spend money. I really should make it a goal to spend $5,000 by the end of this year hiring someone, because I can't do it alone. Yes, I'm a solopreneur and I want to do it, but I'm also kind of lazy and I want the best outcome with the least effort. I know now that nothing really exists on autopilot, but I'm looking for something as autopilot as possible that can replace my income, so that it never really feels like I have to work a day in my life. I mean, I already feel that half the days.

I really struggle from sitting at a desk all day, and I really want to work mobility and strength specifically for people who sit and work at a desk and a computer all day — especially the technical folks who sit and code all day. Posture doesn't even matter when you're static for 8 hours a day. It requires movement and mobility. Use it or lose it. If your body is stuck like this for a third of your life, it's going to have consequences.
