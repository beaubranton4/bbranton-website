Finally had a decent working session yesterday for about an hour on Dugout Edge. I think the gameday tool is ready to launch. In fact, I'm procrastinating its launch. It's been about two months coming — I think it's two months overdue. I really just need to push this thing out there, get some feedback, and I can always iterate once it's out. But if I work two months on a tool, three months, four months, five months on a tool, and always hear crickets, nobody uses it — what was the point? Right, so I need to get it out there. Don't worry too much about the notifications, emails; that can all come later. I just need to get it out, make sure it's working.

I've kind of lost sight of what it means to ship fast. I've become a perfectionist again. And I think it's because I feel that the stakes are higher now that I have 200-something customers, I have people actually looking at my site, I have people that I've talked to. And I really shouldn't be letting that get in the way of learning how to iterate and try and fail fast.

So that's something that I really need to be working on in the coming days, the coming months, the coming years. I need to learn how to fail fast, because that's the quickest way for me to learn.
