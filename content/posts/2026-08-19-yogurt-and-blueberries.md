# August 19, 2026

Today I'm journaling while stretching — multitasking, in a sense, which is great. I really do think the process of journaling through voice is great because it lets you journal while doing other things — while stretching, while walking. I just need to figure out a better form factor to record these journal entries when I'm not near my computer. And I think that's what I'd like to build the second brain for: so I can record something and it automatically gets saved to my second brain, which is really just a project, a database, and a bunch of code that can be controlled through Claude Code, edited and modified via natural language — that's where the huge unlock comes in. I don't need to tediously log anything. I can just speak about what I did today, what I ate, what I need to get done, and it'll all be nicely and neatly formatted for me in a beautiful visual way — easy to recall what I've done in the past, what I need to do going forward, with the additional ability to view things in a more traditional format if I need to — in text, in tables and charts. That's kind of the beauty of this natural-language-powered second brain: I don't have to reformat my thinking, I just need to think in words. I don't have to learn multiple mediums, I just speak.

The tough part is that she's really non-verbal, and being there in person doesn't really do much except affirm to the family that I'm there for them. So it's, again, kind of a weird spot to be in.
