# August 2, 2026

Had an awesome, super memorable weekend just hanging out with my parents. Saturday we got an awesome lunch — fish at Scoma's with Uncle Ricardo and Auntie Patty. I probably already wrote about that, not sure. Did some drop-offs and returns at Macy's. Took them to Strawberry Hill. Then got Hook Fish Co and watched the sunset at Ocean Beach on one of the sand dunes over there. Had some nice alone time.

Then Sunday was the big trip. Went all the way to Point Reyes. Checked out the shipwreck, which was extremely underwhelming — would not recommend, skip that one. Did the radio building tree-lined driveway — the cypress tree tunnel, I don't even know what to call it, but that was pretty cool. Super picturesque, beautiful trees. Had a really good sandwich from — I think it's called Whale of a Deli — in Point Reyes Station. The tri-tip sandwich was fire.

But the real fire part of the day was going to the lighthouse. A lighthouse that is apparently the second windiest and foggiest place in North America, which is pretty incredible. They close the 300-step staircase that lets you walk all the way down and actually touch the lighthouse if winds are over 40 miles per hour — and this July, 17 days had an average wind speed over 40 miles per hour, so the stairs were closed a lot. So we got super lucky. It was a beautiful day. Saw some sea lions. Hiked back up. Went to Chimney Rock Trail and did the mile-out, mile-in hike. Saw some awesome views and sea lions and birds, and it was just really enjoyable. Had to drive back, hung out at Corte Madera for a little bit, did some browsing, picked up Abby, and called it a day.
