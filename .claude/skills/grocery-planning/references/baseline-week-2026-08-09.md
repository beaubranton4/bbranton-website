## Lessons this shop validated

- **Protein purchases are self-limiting; snackable carbs are not.** The overeat risk was concentrated in ~2,800 cal of grazeables: the sourdough loaf (~1,700), rice snaps, and 2.8 lb of cherries. Cut the rice snaps first; the loaf must last 5 days (≈2 slices/day).
- **Protein landed at ~140 g/day — at the aim.** Don't reduce protein buys; a "smaller shop" should shrink the carb column, not the meat counter.
- **Sodium carriers on a typical shop:** smoked salmon, feta, lupini (brined), deli sourdough. Don't stack them into one meal; Beau's limit is 1,500 mg/day.
- **Split-dinner math:** protein-anchored dinners (salmon/shrimp/steak) at ~1 lb serve both people once each; Beau's half of each ≈ 340/200/510 cal.
- **~$190/week** is the validated price point for 5 weekdays × 3 meals for Beau plus 3 shared dinners.
