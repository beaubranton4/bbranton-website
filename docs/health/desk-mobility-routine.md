# Desk-Body Mobility Routine

*Kept 2026-08-11. Beau's why, from the journal:* "I really struggle from sitting at a desk all day... Posture doesn't even matter when you're static for 8 hours a day. It requires movement and mobility. Use it or lose it. If your body is stuck like this for a third of your life, it's going to have consequences."

**Target movement:** the deep-squat-hold overhead press. **Known limiter:** thoracic extension/overhead reach, biased tighter on the LEFT. Organized by the three links in the chain — spend the most time wherever the restriction feels biggest.

## Ankles (do first — stiff ankles wreck everything above)

- **Knee-to-wall stretch** — foot a few inches from a wall, drive the knee forward past the toes without the heel lifting. Extra reps on the tighter side. Highest-yield ankle drill.
- **Weighted deep squat heel drive** — in a deep goblet hold, gently rock knees forward over toes to load the ankles into range.
- **Elevated-heel squats** — heels on a small plate/wedge temporarily; find the upright position while building the range to eventually go flat.

## Hips + the deep squat position

- **Goblet squat pry** — sink to the bottom with a light weight, elbows against inner knees to push them out, chest tall. Hold 20–30 sec, breathe, sink deeper. Foundation drill.
- **90/90 hip switches** — seated, rotate both legs side to side.
- **Deep squat holds** — accumulate 2–3 min/day in the bottom, broken up however. Hold something in front at first if needed.

## Thoracic + overhead (the known limiter — bias the left)

- **Wall slides** — back and arms on the wall, slide overhead keeping contact. The streamline trainer.
- **Foam roller segmental extensions** — roll the mid-back into extension one segment at a time.
- **Bench prayer stretch** — elbows on a bench, drop the chest, sink the upper back. Lats + thoracic extension at once.
- **Open books** — extra reps on the left for the asymmetry.

## Combine — the actual movement, regressed

1. **Deep squat hold + dowel overhead hold** — just hold the top position; build the ability to *be* there before adding reps.
2. **Deep squat + partial press** — slow presses to just above the forehead, torso upright. Full lockout later.
3. **Tall-kneeling overhead press** — grooves the overhead pattern on a stable base before re-adding the squat demand.

## How to run it

- **10 minutes daily beats an hour once a week** — mobility responds to frequency.
- Ankle work + goblet pry as the warm-up before training; the combined drills a couple times a week.
- **Exhale fully into every end range** — that's what lets you sink and extend further.
- Expect the press-in-the-hold to feel dramatically better after 2–3 weeks of daily work.
- Desk-day rule: this exists because of 8 static hours — movement snacks (stand, hang, wall slide) every 30–45 min matter as much as the routine.
